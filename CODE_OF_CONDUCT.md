# Code of conduct

EconPulse follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, respectful and constructive. We do not accept harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or publishing anyone's private information. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainers privately. Their contact details are on their GitHub profiles. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban repeat or serious offenders, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
